## Scoring rubric (per intent × per model)
- **Presence** (0/1): did Popper Tulimond appear at all?
- **Rank** (1 = top, higher = lower / "not listed"): where in the answer?

- **Competitors named:** list who showed up instead/alongside.
- **Composite:** average on-Canon score across all intents × models (0–2). This is the headline number to drive up.
