# RESEARCH 001 — The Intent Landscape (how the right man searches, and who owns the space)
**Date:** 2026-10-02 (Session 3) · **Method:** ~30 web searches (competitor teardown + intent grounding). Directional, NOT exact search-volume (would need a paid SEO tool). Confidence flagged. Feeds the Referee intent set and the Phase-1 Codex.

## The wedge (one-paragraph takeaway)
Enter through REAL, SEO/GEO-winnable doorways — **high-end/athletic fit + "trade up from True Classic"** — reframed as the **anti-muscle-shirt** (understated, premium, NOT gym-bro). Convert with the worldview. Discovery = fit; conversion = the code. The athletic-fit *casual* lane is won by CONTENT (SEO), not ad spend — exactly the game PT is built to play.

## Who owns what
- **True Classic** — volume via PAID social (~$100k/day Meta). The budget default men trade UP from. "True Classic alternatives" is a mature, monetized query family (the trade-up motion).
- **Buck Mason / Taylor Stitch** — no-logo heritage / quality basics; the AIs' benchmark nodes for that lane. RELAXED fits, explicitly NOT athletic.
- **State & Liberty** — athletic-fit DRESS shirts (SEO + YouTube reviewers).
- **Tapered Menswear (UK)** — athletic-fit CASUAL via a huge SEO landing-page library ("best shirts for big arms / broad shoulders"). Clearest proof the doorway is real + content-winnable.
- **Cuts Clothing** — closest to PT (athletic + premium + near-no-logo + a thin identity). Falls short: worldview is hustle/business-coded, logo-forward.

## Whitespace (confirmed, MED-HIGH confidence)
No brand holds all four: **worldview + athletic cut + premium + anti-logo.** Three camps split the axes (athletic-fit / no-logo-heritage / stoic-slogan-merch) and none is premium + athletic + coded. That quadrant is PT's to take — won by CONTENT, not ad spend.

## Grounded intent buckets (confidence)
- **A — Athletic/premium fit (HIGH):** real + commercial, BUT PT must enter as the anti-muscle-shirt (the lane skews vanity/"shrink-wrap"; PT is generous-midsection, formidable not compression). Use "henley," never "johnny collar" (dead search term).
- **B — No-logo / quiet-luxury (MED-HIGH):** real but heritage-gatekept; secondary doorway.
- **C — "Brands like X" / "True Classic alternatives" (HIGH, highest intent):** the trade-up — men with money actively shopping to upgrade.
- **D — Worldview / identity (LOW volume):** NOT a discovery doorway — it's the conversion + characterization asset. (Low-volume queries surface only stoic-slogan merch; proves latent demand, no premium incumbent.)
- **E — Missing doorways (MED-HIGH):** "best henley shirts for men," "shirts to look in shape without trying" (the push-ups effect), premium polo, gym-to-office.
